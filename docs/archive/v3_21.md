# v3.21 — WebRTC AEC3-Alignment Cycle (historical record)

Consolidated reference for the v3.21 arc. Status: **CLOSED** (branch
`v3_21_release`, byte-equal, no algorithm change vs production). Superseded
by v3.22.

## What the arc was

v3.21 was a **strict WebRTC AEC3-alignment cycle**: port/align our PBFDKF +
AEC3-post chain to AEC3 production defaults. Hard scope rule — anything with no
AEC3-default-production equivalent was pushed to v3.22 (beyond-AEC3
optimisation). At v3.21.6.1, **22/22** alignment flags with config presence
shipped ON and were hard-coded into call sites; 6 remained OFF (each documented
with a re-evaluation gate). The 2026-05-28 audit found ~80% of the "still
missing" AEC3 items were already aligned, no-consumer additive surfaces, or
no-ops under our steady state (`current_size = max_size = 13`).

## The two load-bearing substrate bugs fixed by v3.21.6

These are the real wins of the arc and are **load-bearing** (must not regress):

1. **PBFDKF missing `H_error` conditional refresh → P-collapse.**
   Our PBFDKF was missing AEC3's `H_error += factor * erl` conditional refresh
   (`refined_filter_update_gain.cc:128-138`). Without it, `P` collapses and the
   Kalman gain dies on hard cases. Adding it lifted `9xjhi` echo
   **10.22 → 18.64 dB** (beating AEC3 ref 17.6 by ~+1 dB). Prior cycles' "we are
   better than AEC3" framing was on a *different* Pareto point, not strictly
   better — this fix corrected that.

2. **ERLE convergence-gate latch → per-frame `e²<0.5y²` rule.**
   Root cause of v3.20/v3.21 weak echo cancellation: `bridge.filter_converged`
   was sourced from a legacy strict 10-frame ERLE latch that stays permanently
   `False` on hard cases → `SubbandErleEstimator` update gated off → ERLE stuck
   at `min_erle=1.0` → R²=S² → `SuppressionGain` effectively blind. Fix: compute
   the AEC3-style **per-frame `e² < 0.5·y²`** convergence rule inside `_aec3_post`.

## Tier-C validation outcome / v3.21 CLOSE

A hop/fft conversion audit + 800-case Tier-C validation adjudicated every
"physical-meaning conversion" flag under matched-magnitude AECMOS Pareto (less
echo auto-lifts deg — that alone is *not* a win). Surviving default-True
alignment flags were then inlined into their call sites and all NOSHIP / temp
substrate deleted. The close was **byte-equal** (`_ours` + `_ours_nores` md5,
all buckets incl. movement).

Cleanup magnitude on the two large files:
- config **412 → 230**, filters **1057 → 863** (legacy P-denominator Kalman body
  removed; 10 always-True refined/shadow AEC3-parity flags inlined).
- orchestrator construction / `_aec3_post` branches collapsed.
- removed unused alternate filter line (`NlmsFilter` + `AecMode.LMS`/`NLMS`) and
  dead `erle.py` back-compat re-export.
- `active_render` 5.96e-4 documented as empirically-tuned (strict-AEC3 9.31e-6
  validated as a regression) — the only flag whose "alignment" label changed.

`v3_21_release` was finalised on this branch and **not merged to main**; main
carries the v3.22 line. The `PathChangeRegimeHandler` (formerly
`ShadowCopyController`) is load-bearing on the cohort tail (~7/800) and was kept.
